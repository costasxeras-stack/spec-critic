    How it works

    • The instant check is plain JavaScript: it looks for headings such as problem, users, success metrics, non-goals, risks and open questions, counts acceptance criteria, and highlights vague words like "intuitive" or "TBD".
    • The AI review sends your spec and a fixed reviewer prompt to the Claude Messages API and asks for JSON: six scores with reasons, issues with an exact quote from the spec, questions and a rewrite.
    • Every quote the model returns is checked against your text. A quote that does not appear word for word is flagged as "not found in spec", so a made-up quote cannot pass as real.
    • Nothing is stored on a server. There is no server.
